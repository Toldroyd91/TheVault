Your Survey Diagnostics

Explore exactly how your new living space is being engineered. We believe in total transparency regarding your property's structural requirements.

Build Type & Size

-

This defines the footprint of your new space. Engineered to maximize your property's potential while respecting boundary limits.

Roof System & Pitch

-

The pitch ensures optimal rain runoff, while the system itself dictates the thermal performance and internal ceiling height.

External Frame

-

Chosen to either seamlessly match your existing home's aesthetic or provide a striking modern contrast.

Internal Frame

-

Dictates the interior décor feel. Often kept white to maximize light reflection and create a spacious atmosphere.

Base Construction

-

Determined by your local soil geology and load-bearing requirements to guarantee a lifetime of structural integrity.

DPC Alignment

-

Damp Proof Course measurement. Crucial for perfectly sealing the new structure against your home to prevent any moisture bridging.

Sub-Floor Venting

-

Ensures your existing home continues to breathe correctly beneath the floorboards once the new extension is attached.

Fascia Height

-

Calculated to ensure the new roofline clears your existing first-floor windows while maximizing interior headroom.

Access Route

-

Surveyed to plan the safe transport of heavy materials (like steel beams and glass) from the road to your garden.

Drainage Works

-

Maps existing manholes and pipes. If your new base crosses a drain, we safely reroute it to maintain your utility access.

Wall Obstacles

-

Identifies boiler flues, extractor fans, or soil pipes that need careful working around or relocation during the build phase.

Building Control

-

Yorkshire Windows handles all necessary regulatory certificates directly with inspectors to ensure your build is legally sound.

Planning Permission

-

Determines if your build falls under Permitted Development or requires a formal application. We process this for you.

SAP Energy Calcs

-

Thermal loss calculations. This proves to Building Control that your new space will remain highly energy efficient, keeping you warm in winter.
